# Project Scope

## Purpose

Know Nepal is a digital knowledge platform focused on Nepal.

The project aims to bring structured and accessible information about
Nepal into one platform, allowing users to explore, learn about, and
discover different aspects of the country.

The platform currently covers multiple knowledge domains including
geography, education, destinations, culture, history, wildlife, and
healthcare.

---

## In Scope

The current scope of Know Nepal includes:

### 1. Nepal Knowledge

Providing structured information about Nepal across multiple domains:

- Geography
- Education
- Destinations
- Culture
- History
- Wildlife
- Healthcare

### 2. Exploration and Discovery

Users can:

- Explore information about different places and subjects
- Browse destinations and related information
- Discover structured knowledge about Nepal
- Navigate between related information where supported

### 3. Structured Data

The platform maintains structured domain data rather than treating
the website as a collection of static articles.

This includes domain entities, relationships, metadata, and
supporting information required by the application.

### 4. Itineraries and Travel Planning

Users can explore destinations and create personalized itineraries
with Know Nepal AI for their travel planning.

An AI-assisted trip-planning capability may be used to generate
personalized itineraries grounded in Know Nepal's available data.

### 5. Public Documentation

The Know Nepal ecosystem includes public documentation describing:

- Project purpose and scope
- System architecture
- Development practices
- APIs and shared concepts
- Contribution guidelines
- Architectural decisions

---

## Out of Scope

Know Nepal is not intended to be:

- A replacement for official government information
- A real-time emergency or medical response service
- A booking platform for flights, hotels, or tours
- A payment platform
- A social-media platform
- A general-purpose global knowledge platform
- A source of guaranteed real-time information unless explicitly
  provided by an integrated source

Where information may change over time, users should verify
important information with the relevant official or authoritative
source.

---

## Scope Boundaries

Know Nepal focuses specifically on **knowledge, exploration, and
discovery related to Nepal**.

The platform may integrate additional capabilities in the future,
but new functionality should support this core purpose rather than
turning Know Nepal into an unrelated general-purpose platform.

---

## Current vs Future Scope

The scope described above represents the overall direction of the
project.

Individual capabilities may be:

- **Implemented** — currently available and supported in the platform
- **In Progress** — currently being developed
- **Planned** — intended for future development
- **Historical** — previously explored but no longer part of the current architecture

Know Nepal is developed **incrementally and carefully**, with changes made as the project evolves.
The implementation and source code remain the source of truth for what is currently available.
