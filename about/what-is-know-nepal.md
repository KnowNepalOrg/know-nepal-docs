# What is Know Nepal?

Know Nepal is a digital platform focused on organizing, preserving, and making information about Nepal more accessible.

The platform brings together information about different aspects of Nepal, including its geography, history, culture, destinations, wildlife, education, and healthcare.

Rather than treating these areas as completely separate sources of information, Know Nepal aims to bring them together as part of one connected knowledge platform.

---

## What Does Know Nepal Cover?

The platform is organized around several major areas of knowledge:

- **Geography** — Information about Nepal's geographical structure and places.
- **History** — Historical information, events, figures, and periods.
- **Culture** — Information about Nepal's cultural diversity, traditions, festivals, languages, and arts.
- **Destinations** — Information about places, attractions, and destinations across Nepal.
- **Wildlife** — Information about Nepal's wildlife, flora, biodiversity, and protected areas.
- **Education** — Information related to educational institutions, programs, examinations, and opportunities.
- **Healthcare** — Information related to healthcare institutions and services.

These areas may continue to grow and evolve as the platform develops.

---

## Why Know Nepal Exists

Information about Nepal is often spread across many different sources.

Finding reliable and useful information can therefore require searching through multiple websites, documents, and other resources.

Know Nepal aims to make this information easier to discover by bringing relevant knowledge together in one organized platform.

The goal is not simply to collect information, but to organize it in a way that makes it easier for people to explore and understand Nepal.

---

## Who Is Know Nepal For?

Know Nepal is intended for anyone interested in learning about Nepal.

This can include:

- Students
- Researchers
- Travelers
- Teachers
- Developers
- Contributors
- Organizations
- People interested in Nepal and its heritage

The platform is designed to serve both people who already know Nepal well and people who are discovering it for the first time.

---

## A Growing Knowledge Platform

Know Nepal is an evolving project.

New information, domains, features, and improvements may be introduced over time.

As the platform grows, its documentation and organization will evolve alongside it.

The current documentation describes the project based on its present direction and implementation.

---

## More Than a Website

Know Nepal is intended to be more than a collection of web pages.

It is being developed as a structured knowledge platform where information can be organized by domain and gradually improved over time.

The long-term goal is to create a useful and maintainable foundation for exploring knowledge about Nepal.

---

## Scope

The current focus of Know Nepal is Nepal-related knowledge and information.

The platform may expand its scope in the future, but its central purpose remains:

> **Making knowledge about Nepal easier to discover, explore, and understand.**
