# Product

This section describes what Know Nepal is building, who it is intended
for, and how the product is structured from a user perspective.

## Purpose

Know Nepal is a digital knowledge platform focused on making structured
information about Nepal more accessible, discoverable, and reusable.

The platform brings together information across areas such as:

- Geography
- History
- Culture
- Wildlife
- Education
- Healthcare
- Destinations

## Users

Know Nepal is intended to serve people who need reliable and structured
information about Nepal, including:

- People exploring Nepal
- Travelers planning trips
- Students and researchers
- Developers and open-data users
- People looking for information about Nepal's places, culture, and
  public resources

## Core Experience

The product is organized around three primary experiences:

### Discover

Explore structured information about Nepal through different
knowledge domains.

### Explore

Navigate deeper into places, topics, destinations, and related
information.

### Plan

Use destination information and the AI trip planner to create
personalized travel plans based on user requirements.

## Product Principles

Know Nepal aims to:

- Prefer structured information over scattered content
- Make information easier to discover and understand
- Keep domain information organized and reusable
- Connect knowledge with practical use cases
- Build on open software and open-data principles

## Current Product Scope

The current platform focuses on structured knowledge about Nepal and
travel-related discovery and planning.

The product is under active development, and its scope may evolve as
new use cases and user needs are validated.

## Product Direction

Future product decisions should be guided by:

- Real user needs
- Usage and feedback
- Data quality
- Information discoverability
- Practical usefulness
- Sustainable development of the platform
